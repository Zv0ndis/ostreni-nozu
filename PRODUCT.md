## Platform

web

## Users
A broad local audience in Czechia: home cooks with dull kitchen knives, professional cooks, and owners of Japanese or premium knives. They want a knife that cuts again, a clear price, and an easy way to hand knives over.

## Product Purpose
One-page site for a one-person hand-sharpening service. Success: a visitor understands what gets sharpened, what it costs, and how to hand knives over, then calls or e-mails.

## Positioning
Sharpening by hand on water stones (#240 to #6000), no belt grinder, so the steel never overheats. Edge angle matched to the knife (about 15° Japanese, 20° European).

## Operating Context
Hand-off in person by arrangement or by post (knives packed in cardboard, edge inward). Usual turnaround 3 working days. Payment cash or bank transfer. Hours Mon-Fri 16-19, Sat by arrangement.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Services and prices (CZK): European kitchen knife 120, Japanese knife 250, serrated 150, pocket/hunting 120, scissors 150, chisel/plane iron 150, chip or tip repair from 100. 10 % off from 5 knives. Prices indicative.
- Static site, single `index.html`, hosted on GitHub Pages; custom domain to be added later.
- Language: Czech.

- Craft is shown through the process (stones, angles), not claimed with adjectives.
