# Surface: index.html (one-page site)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: mixed local (home cooks, cooks, Japanese-knife owners). Action: call or e-mail to arrange sharpening. Proof: process (stones, angles), prices. No photos, no reviews; placeholders for contact.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a title sequence for a dull knife's trip back to sharp: flat cut-paper blade, stone and shard shapes on flat colour cards, one idea per card. Refuses the category default of photo hero, price table and three equal step cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cardinal orange field, ink black, bone stock, olive, midnight, ochre. Skinny condensed caps set slightly off baseline (League Gothic), Barlow for reading text. Black cut-paper silhouettes with slight rotation, fixed paper grain, a jittering hand-drawn line marks the live element. Square corners, no shadows, no cards with borders.

STORY: Visitor sees the blade meet the stone, reads what gets sharpened and what it costs, watches the grit sequence go coarse to fine, learns the hand-off, calls.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed cardinal field. Wordmark top-left, three links top-right. Left 60%: headline "Nože, co zase řežou." in huge bone skinny caps, one sentence under it, ink "Zavolat" tel button plus outline "Ceník". Right: black cut-paper chef's knife crossing out of frame diagonally, edge resting on a bone whetstone, jitter line along the edge.

FORM: Saul Bass title sequence, dealt challenger (not on my grounded list, which ranked: repair ticket, water stones, painted shop sign, letter-board price list, edge micrographs, catalogue silhouettes, angle gauge). Seed key 32f9cae5. Signature interaction: grit cards #240 to #6000 cut in staccato on scroll, shards getting finer per card (density raise from the ASCII challenger).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
